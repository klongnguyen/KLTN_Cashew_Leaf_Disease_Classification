# EXP-030 protocol — DenseNet121 real-image oversampling

**Question.** Does repeating each of the 294 `real_*` Train images four times improve the mean accuracy on real Holdout 09? EXP-029 reached Test 96.2336%, Holdout 82.6984%, and a 13.5352-point gap. EXP-030 changes `REAL_OVERSAMPLE_FACTOR` from 1 to 4. This yields 5,854 Train samples, 366 steps per epoch at batch 16, and a 20.1% `real_*` share. Validation, Test, and Holdout are never oversampled.

**Selection bookkeeping.** The primary Validation selection rule returns to `val_loss`. In EXP-029 the `real_subgroup_accuracy` rule tied on all five seeds (51/59 each) and `aggregate/selection_comparison.json` reports `"rules_agree": true`, so the revert is expected to change no checkpoint. No Holdout outcome selects a checkpoint, seed, or another oversampling factor.

**Frozen recipe.** Split (13) ZIP SHA256: `e1647b72e10af04275dc760545f4ef17c9f44343ae119495b89ba1e3cd954e04`; Train/Validation/Test: 4,972/1,452/685. Holdout 09: 126 images (anthracnose 46, healthy 19, leaf_miner 36, red_rust 25). Classes: anthracnose, healthy, leaf_miner, red_rust, not_cashew_leaf. Five seeds: 42, 123, 2026, 3407, 7777. DenseNet121 ImageNet backbone, 256 px raw RGB input, the EXP-029 training-only photometric augmentation, dropout 0.40, AdamW, batch 16, two stages, frozen BatchNorm, and all learning rates, patience values, and epoch caps remain as in EXP-029.

**Abort gates before training.** Verify the pinned dataset ZIP hash, split and class counts, Holdout archive size and counts, 294 `real_*` Train images, 5,854 repeated Train rows, 366 steps, 20.0–20.2% real share, and unchanged evaluation row counts. Compare decoded-pixel SHA256 and 64-bit pHash for every Holdout image against every `real_*` Train image. Exact pixel overlap aborts; pHash Hamming distance ≤4 is written to `leakage_audit.json` for review. The inherited photometric, inference-identity, Stage 2 parameter (2,129,920), and frozen BatchNorm (121 layers) smoke gates must pass. The embedded EXP-029 per-image reference table is SHA256 checked and its published means reproduced.

**Endpoints and interpretation.** The primary endpoint is five-seed mean Holdout accuracy and its paired 10,000-resample image-bootstrap 95% interval versus EXP-029. Report per-seed Test, Holdout, gap, paired t-tests, pooled Holdout per-class recall and confusion matrix, predicted-anthracnose share of Holdout errors, Validation accuracy/macro-F1, and Holdout excluding the two named images (n=124) for both experiments. Report whether the gap is below 10 points, but do not use that alone as success: at n=126, one image changes accuracy by 0.79 points and sampling uncertainty is large. Print **TEST REGRESSION** if mean Test accuracy drops more than 1.0 point versus EXP-029. Every Holdout figure is labeled **descriptive audit, not a blind benchmark**; Holdout 09 was historically exposed. A different oversampling factor requires a new experiment ID.

**Known issue.** Holdout `healthy/real_healthy_40.jpg` and `healthy/real_healthy_42.jpg` are byte-identical to `real_anthracnose_157.jpg` and `real_anthracnose_159.jpg` from the earlier Split (12) Train set. Split (13) removed those and `real_anthracnose_155.jpg` from Train; this experiment never trains on them. Their true labels are unverified. Keep their ground truth as `healthy`, neither drop nor relabel them, and report the n=124 secondary metric.
